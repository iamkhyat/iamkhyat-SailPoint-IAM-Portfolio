# Certifications

## SailPoint Certifications

All five certifications below are issued by SailPoint and are current.

| Certification | Focus Area |
|---|---|
| SailPoint IdentityIQ Engineer | IIQ implementation, connectors, provisioning, workflows |
| SailPoint IdentityIQ Professional | Advanced IIQ — lifecycle, certifications, RBAC architecture |
| SailPoint Identity Security Leader | IAM program strategy, governance, risk frameworks |
| SailPoint Identity Security Professional | Cross-platform identity security, SoD, access governance |
| SailPoint Identity Security Expert | Advanced architecture, IDN + IIQ, enterprise IAM design |

## What these cover in practice

The engineer and professional certifications validate the hands-on IIQ work — connectors, provisioning rules, lifecycle automation, certification campaigns. The security-track certifications (Leader, Professional, Expert) go broader into program design, governance frameworks, and cross-platform architecture, which is closer to where I'm targeting my career next.

## Verify

SailPoint certifications are verifiable through the [SailPoint community portal](https://community.sailpoint.com). Happy to share badge links directly on request.
